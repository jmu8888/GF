参与第二十五届文化节Be Part of the 25th Festival

历经二十余届的华盛顿中国文化节，不仅是一场文化的盛宴，更是一座沟通心灵、连接世界的桥梁。欢迎社团、商家、艺术团体和每一位朋友加入。

After more than two decades, the festival is not only a celebration of culture but a bridge between hearts and nations. Associations, businesses, performers and friends are all welcome to take part.
