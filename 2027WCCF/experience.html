Culture Experience

文化体验展区Cultural Experience Booths

华盛顿特区市长在贺信中说，华盛顿中国文化节通过多种活动展示中国文化，“可以让年轻人去感受文化差异、产生好奇心并且更好地了解中国文化”。现场设置八十多个展位，多角度、立体化展示中国文化。

In a congratulatory letter, the Mayor of Washington, D.C. noted that the festival lets young people experience cultural differences, spark curiosity and better understand Chinese culture. More than eighty booths present Chinese culture from many angles.

🖌️

书法与国画Calligraphy & Painting

现场挥毫，写下自己的中文名字或一句祝福。

Write your Chinese name or a blessing with brush and ink.

🎭

画脸谱Opera Masks

描绘京剧脸谱，认识红忠、黑直、白奸的色彩寓意。

Paint a Peking Opera mask and learn what each color means.

🧸

捏面人与剪纸Dough Figures & Paper-cut

非遗手艺人现场演示，亲手体验传统民间工艺。

Folk artisans demonstrate intangible heritage crafts you can try.

⚫

围棋Game of Go

与棋友对弈，一探黑白之间的东方智慧。

Play a round of Go and discover the strategy behind the stones.

☯️

太极拳Tai Chi

跟随老师练太极，感受天人合一的东方哲学。

Learn Tai Chi from instructors and feel its harmony of body and mind.

👘

旗袍与民族服饰Qipao & Ethnic Dress

各地展区带来苗族银饰、旗袍等民族服饰展示。

Regional booths showcase Miao silverwork, qipao and other traditional dress.
