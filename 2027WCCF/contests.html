华盛顿中国文化节摄影大赛Festival Photography Contest

记录文化节现场的精彩瞬间。第二十三届摄影大赛由维州摄影俱乐部、美京摄影俱乐部、西北摄影友社和北美熊猫摄影协会共同协办，评出一等奖1个、二等奖2个、三等奖3个、优秀奖10个。

Capturing the festival’s best moments. The 23rd festival contest was co-organized by four regional photography clubs and awarded 1 first prize, 2 second prizes, 3 third prizes and 10 honorable mentions.

青少年作文大赛Junior Essay Competition

由载道网站 thez.us 承办，全美中文学校协会担任指导单位。2025年大赛以“文化的桥，连着你和我”为主题，共收到138份中英文投稿，其中98位同学进入决赛，并首次在大华府地区举行现场决赛。

Hosted by thez.us with guidance from the National Council of Associations of Chinese Language Schools. The 2025 theme, “A Bridge of Culture Connecting You and Me,” drew 138 Chinese and English entries; 98 students reached the first in-person final in Greater Washington.

文化节义工Festival Volunteers

文化节的成功举办离不开每一位义工无私的奉献。每一位义工都是“真诚、奉献、友爱、互助”精神的践行者，将不同肤色、不同族裔的人们连接起来，为了一个共同的美好目标：传承和发扬中华文化。

The festival depends on the selfless work of its volunteers. Each one lives the spirit of sincerity, dedication, friendship and mutual help, connecting people of every background around one shared goal: carrying Chinese culture forward.
