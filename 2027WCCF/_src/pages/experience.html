文化体验展区Cultural Experience Booths

华盛顿特区市长在贺信中说，华盛顿中国文化节通过多种活动展示中国文化，“可以让年轻人去感受文化差异、产生好奇心并且更好地了解中国文化”。现场设置八十多个展位，多角度、立体化展示中国文化。

In a congratulatory letter, the Mayor of Washington, D.C. noted that the festival lets young people experience cultural differences, spark curiosity and better understand Chinese culture. More than eighty booths present Chinese culture from many angles.
