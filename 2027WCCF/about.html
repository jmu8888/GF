About the Festival

华盛顿中国文化节简介Washington Chinese Culture Festival

自1999年起，由华盛顿地区多个华侨华人社团联合主办的华盛顿中国文化节，至2027年将迎来第二十五届。二十多年来，文化节以弘扬中华文化、促进中美民间交流为宗旨，日益发展成美国首都地区最具影响力、最具规模的中华传统文化大型展示活动，被誉为华盛顿地区一张闪亮的“文化名片”。

Since its inception in 1999, the Washington Chinese Culture Festival, jointly organized by various Chinese community associations in the Washington metropolitan area, will reach its 25th edition in 2027. Over two decades, it has become the most influential and large-scale cultural event showcasing traditional Chinese heritage in the U.S. capital, serving as a shining “cultural landmark” of Washington, D.C.

文化节的意义与影响Cultural Significance and Impact

华盛顿中国文化节致力于展示中华文明的独特魅力，为美国民众了解中国、认识中国、感受中国搭建平台。活动期间，游客可品尝地道的中华美食、欣赏多彩的文艺表演、体验非遗手工艺，近距离感受中国传统与现代文化的融合之美。经过二十余年的发展，文化节不仅成为华盛顿地区的文化盛事，更成为促进中美人民相互理解与友谊的重要桥梁。

The festival serves as a dynamic platform for American audiences to explore the depth and diversity of Chinese civilization. Visitors can enjoy authentic Chinese cuisine, experience traditional arts and crafts, and appreciate cultural performances that blend ancient heritage with modern creativity. After more than two decades, the festival has become a key cultural event in the Greater Washington, D.C. area and an important bridge fostering mutual understanding and friendship between the peoples of China and the United States.
